# Quota-stop

Idle-wake inspects the visible screen once for a named worker with an active local job. It records quota decisions locally in the daemon database and writes an hk note at `quota-stop/<wake-hash>`; no screen text is stored. The `policy/quota-stop` hk document supplies the four Markdown values in its `## Values` section. The cached document is refreshed at most every five minutes and remains available across restarts. If neither source is usable, the decision is handover.

Each observation has three bounds: 2 s for finding the pane's job and reserving the wake, 5 s shared by the screen read and herdr's `agent explain` lookup (the lookup runs after the read and gets only the time the read left, so it never adds a second timeout), and 5 s for the policy and task lookups a quota screen needs. Every read screen logs one `quota-stop observation` line with `outcome` (`quota`, `not_quota`, `screen_read_timeout`, `observation_canceled` or `screen_read_failed`) and `scan_ms`/`read_ms`; the screen text is never logged. A failed read records a `handover` decision with the same reason: `screen_read_timeout` is the read's own deadline, `observation_canceled` the caller's cancellation, `screen_read_failed` the read command's error.

Detection is herdr-first. The observation asks `herdr agent explain <pane> --json` for the matched rule and the agent kind; the top-level `agent` and `matched_rule` keys are read as herdr 0.9.5 returns them, and a `result`-wrapped reply is accepted too. A `quota_limit` rule means herdr recognised the stop: the screen text supplies the reset, and when the text has no parseable limit the pool comes from the agent kind with the reset unknown, handed over as `reset_unknown`. Otherwise the screen classifier decides. herdr 0.9.5 ships no `quota_limit` rule, so every detection today is the classifier's; the herdr path is covered by fixture only, not by a live quota stop. The basis (`herdr:quota_limit` or `classifier`) is logged on each `quota-stop observation` and `quota-stop action` line; it is not stored in the database.

A candidate whose owner lane is a design lane is suppressed from the idle-wake route, but its screen is still read once, keyed by the `quota_stop_decisions` event id, so a stop on a design-owned worker is decided. Suppressed design-lane candidates are re-scanned each tick until observed; that set is small and its cost is not measured.

The default is shadow (`quota_stop.act=false`, represented by the zero-value `Config.QuotaStopAct`); no pane input or handover event is sent. Set the daemon flag `--quota-stop-act` to enable actions. The daemon account needs `handoffkeep` and its configured credentials, plus `herdr`. Job and pane identity, state, screen, and harness are checked before a single resume attempt. A nonce is appended to the policy resume text to verify its submission. An ambiguous submission is never retried. Dialogs are handed over instead of choosing an option. The operator-desk handover is a durable lane event.

This feature does not change spawn routing. A devin free-tier stop also writes this host's `devin-free-limit.json` (the record `panewire quota observe devin-free-limit` writes); the next devin measurement publishes it to the shared store, and later selections skip the free rung as `free_limit` until its reset. The spawn order is panewire's pool-order policy ([config](config.md), decision/2026-11-04/pool-order-owner and decision/2026-11-11/model-routing-escalation A31).

A blocked pane is never resumed. herdr refuses `agent.prompt` on a blocked pane (`agent_blocked`) and sends nothing, so a quota stop seen on a blocked wake is handed over as `blocked_dialog`, and a due wait whose pane is blocked at resume time is handed over the same way without reading the screen or spending the job's one wait.

## A quota stop, by role

hk 3334. The wait or hand-over rule is hk `policy/quota-stop`; this section is the mechanics. The role split is in [config](config.md) ("A quota-stopped job, by role").

**Which role.** The role is the one the `job.spawned` receipt names (reviewer, tester, worker). The claim records only worker or builder, so it is never used alone (`Job.QuotaRole`, `quotaStopManager.jobFacts`).

**Reviewer.** The job watcher sees an idle reviewer pane on a quota screen with no report. It records `job.lost` with `reason: "quota_stop"`, `pool` and `reset_at`, and leaves the hk record and the quota lease alone for `QuotaStopHandOffGrace` (10 minutes). The hub runs the whole replacement under a hard 6 minute context deadline and gives the successor a 150 s spawn deadline, so it is done, one way or the other, well before the watcher ends the job (the 4 minute margin covers the relay from the watcher to the hub). hk never rewrites a lost record, so the pointer to the successor can only be written together with `state=lost`. The hub, on that `job.lost`, finds the job's stored submission and the pool in its hk record, probes the pane (it must be idle), and submits a successor: same task, role, review pass, owner and brief, a new job id `<old>-q<n>` (`n` counts the replacements already in the id; at most 2), a new agent label and checkout directory, a `--report-relative` that points under the old job moved under the new one, and `--exclude-pool <pool>`. A job that inherited its task (`--parent-job`, no `--task`) is replaced with the same arguments. Before it places anything the hub saves the old pane's screen and closes the pane (`pane.close` re-reads it and refuses a pane that changed, in which case nothing is placed, so one job holds the task), and it checks that the whole `end_reason` fits hk's 128 characters. Once the successor is placed (or, when the wait for it ends with its operation still reconciling or past its deadline, because a late node result can still start it and hk cannot repair the record afterwards: a pointer to a successor that later fails is a recoverable chain, a running successor nobody points to is duplicate work) it has the holder's node write the hk record `state=lost`, `end_reason="quota_stop pool=<pool> replaced_by=<new job>"` (`job.mark_lost` with `end_reason`). `replaced_by` is the last `key=value` token and the id fits `[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,128}` (the mergewake hk 3335 contract, pinned by `TestQuotaStopEndReasonFormatIsPinned`). The hub logs the replacement and sends the owner one info lane event, "replaced <old> (<pool> quota) with <new> on <host>/<profile>". The watcher keeps watching through the grace even past its ordinary completion deadline, and a pane that vanishes during it is not ended by the generic missing-pane path (the hub closed it). A pane that goes back to work during the grace records `job.reprompted` and the job lives on. When the successor cannot be placed (no other pool; the pane is then already closed), or the pane is not idle, or the hub is old or unreachable, the watcher ends the job itself after the grace, leaving a record the hub already wrote alone, as before otherwise: `state=lost`, `end_reason="quota_stop pool=<pool> reset_at=<RFC3339 UTC>"`, quota released.

**Worker and tester.** The watcher does not end the job. The quota-stop manager acts for these two roles even when `--quota-stop-act` is off (it stays observe-only for every other role, and for a decision with no proven quota screen). Its decision (`quotaDecisionJob`): wait when the reset is at or before the job's start plus the task's expected time (hk `--expected`, so there is no default expected time: a task without one is handed over), otherwise hand over; an urgent priority (`policy/quota-stop`) and a second stop after one resume also hand over. A wait resumes the same pane once at the reset with the policy's resume text, nothing else, and sends the owner one info lane event. A hand-over records `job.lost` with `reason: "quota_stop"` and the hub replaces the job as for a reviewer, with the end reason `quota_stop pool=<pool> handed_over_to=<new job>`, the same worktree and branch, and one brief line naming the earlier job's report and branch. The record is written **before** the successor is claimed (the ticket's order, and the spawn refuses a task still bound to a job that is not lost); when the spawn then fails, the pointer names a job that never ran and the owner is told by the spawn failure notice. If the hub cannot even write it, the watcher ends the job after the grace, as for a reviewer. A hand-over also starts the job's watcher when it has already ended (a worker that waited past its completion deadline), and a node restart re-arms a watcher that died during the grace.

A hub restarted between claiming a reviewer's successor and writing the old record is repaired by a periodic pass (`reconcileQuotaReplacements`, every 2 minutes, for successors older than the replacement budget): a successor that placed whose predecessor's record is still live gets the pane closed and the record written with `replaced_by`. A successor that never left `accepted` (no node) can never place a job and gets no pointer; one still reconciling or past its deadline with a node binding does.

Known limits: a worker or tester on another host does not carry uncommitted changes or unpushed commits (the ticket's hand-over is the same worktree path, branch and brief; there is no state transfer), and a pane that resumed and settled idle again between the hub's probe and `pane.close` is closed.

## Job end on a quota stop (no replacement)

When no replacement is made, the job watcher ends a reviewer job whose pane is idle on a quota stop screen and has no report. It writes the hk record `state=lost` with `end_reason="quota_stop pool=<pool> reset_at=<RFC3339 UTC>"` (`reset_at` left out when unknown, at most 128 characters, no `result`), emits `job.lost` with `reason: "quota_stop"`, `pool` and `reset_at`, and releases the quota reservation. It only reads the screen and never sends input to the pane. The classifier is `internal/quotastop`; the devin line is matched anywhere in the status line, including the `▲ Something went wrong — ` prefix. A report with the end signal still takes the done path.

## An exhausted pool

hk 4550, operator decision 248. A pane's quota screen is observed only once the pane is idle, so the jobs on a pool that is already known to be used up would sit on dead panes for hours. The hub therefore acts on the pool's live jobs at once, without a screen observation (`hub_pool_exhausted.go`).

**Trigger.** A node's quota value that the hub records (`publishFleetStateFromNode`, fleet state kind `quota`) and that shows the account used up with its reset still ahead: the free bucket `exhausted` (a devin free-limit record that reached the shared store), or a window of scope `account` (or no scope, and no `model`) at 100 % with a readable `resets_at`. A window of one model group does not count, since the account's other models stay usable. An identical value repeated within a minute is not recorded and does not start a pass.

**Which jobs.** The live hub spawn operations (`liveSpawnTasks`, the rule of `[quota].pool_max_concurrent`) that succeeded and whose placement record, `pool`, `account_key` and `profile` in the operation outcome (written from the executing host's receipt), names that pool and account, and a rung the exhausted bucket pays for. The free bucket pays only for the free-tier rungs (`fleet.IsFreeRung`: the `devin-swe2*` profiles), so a free-limit trigger acts only on those; a used-up paid window acts on the other rungs of the account, not the free ones. An operation with no placement record or profile, another pool, another account, or a rung the bucket does not pay for is never touched.

| Role | Action |
|---|---|
| worker, tester | Handed over through the hand-over above (`replaceQuotaStoppedWhy`): the record ends `handed_over_to`, the successor takes the same worktree and branch with `-m auto`, the exhausted pool excluded |
| reviewer, consult (`counsel`) | Ended now: the pane is saved and closed, the record ends `quota_stop pool=<pool> reset_at=<RFC3339 UTC>`, the role is released, and the owner gets `job.lost` reason `quota_stop` with the pool and reset, so it respawns the job. The hub does not replace it |

Each job's action runs on its own goroutine, all at once. A pane that is not idle, or that the node cannot list, is skipped and tried again the next time the exhaustion is seen; nothing is closed under a working pane. A job whose `hk` record is no longer `claimed` or `spawned` is skipped.

**Records.** One `pool exhausted job` log line per job acted on (`job`, `role`, `pool`, `account`, `cause`, `action`, `successor`), and the job's hk record (`handed_over_to` or the `quota_stop` end reason). One `pool exhausted sweep` line per pool event (`pool`, `account`, `cause`, `reset`, `jobs`, `handed_over`, `ended`, and `skipped` as `reason=count`); a repeat of the same event is logged at debug level unless it acted.

**Idempotent.** A job a pass is acting on or has acted on is held in memory, and its hk record ends, so the same exhaustion seen again, from any host, acts once. A skipped job is released for the next look.
